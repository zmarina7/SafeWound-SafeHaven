# SafeWound & SafeHaven 🛡️Integrated Offline Safety and Mental Health System for Women

SafeWound and SafeHaven is a dual-component system designed to address the critical gaps in emergency response, forensic evidence collection, and mental health support for survivors of gender-based violence (GBV) in Ethiopia.📌 Problem StatementEthiopia faces a significant challenge with uncontained rape and GBV crises. Current limitations include:Connectivity Gaps: Lack of internet/cellular coverage in critical moments.Evidence Loss: Failure to capture forensic data immediately for legal recourse.Cultural Barriers: Lack of mental health support tailored to local languages and traditions.🚀 The Solution1. SafeWound (Hardware Ecosystem)An offline-first safety bracelet designed for immediate intervention and forensic capture.Alert System: 1-click trigger to 991 emergency services and nearby contacts via Bluetooth/High-frequency sound.Forensic Capture: Integrated sensors for attacker fingerprinting and audio/video recording.Connectivity: Utilizes LoRa (Long Range) and satellite communication to ensure functionality in remote areas without GSM coverage.Identity Integration: Uses Fayida ID for verified user and attacker identification.2. SafeHaven (Web-App Ecosystem)A comprehensive mental health platform focused on resilience and recovery.Ehitalem AI: A multilingual chatbot providing immediate emotional support in Amharic, Oromo, Tigrinya, Somali, and Afar.Sound Therapy: Frequency-based audio tools optimized for stress reduction.Secure Journaling: Encrypted diaries and mood tracking for survivors.Resilience Training: Gamified psychological support rooted in Ethiopian cultural proverbs and scales.🛠️ Technical Stack (Proposed)Hardware: LoRaWAN modules, Biometric sensors, ESP32/Arduino.Mobile/Web: React Native or Flutter (for cross-platform access).AI/ML: Natural Language Processing (NLP) for local languages.Database: Encrypted SQL/NoSQL with decentralized storage for forensic data.🗺️ Project Roadmap[ ] 

Phase 1: Finalize hardware circuit design and LoRa gateway testing.[ ]

Phase 2: Develop the "Ehitalem" AI prototype with basic Amharic NLP.[ ]

Phase 3: Pilot hardware with a 100-person focus group.[ ] 

Phase 4: Official integration with Ministry of Health and 991 dispatch systems.

⚖️ Ethical Considerations & PrivacyData security is our priority. All forensic evidence and personal diaries are end-to-end encrypted. We aim to comply with national data protection standards to ensure survivor anonymity and legal integrity of evidence.
